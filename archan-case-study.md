# Arc Chan ($ARCHAN): bounded public case study

Research observations: 2026-09-27, approximately 23:53–23:58 KST (UTC+9), with tracker and X values treated as snapshots. This record does not establish a return, an organic launch, or a causal marketing effect.

## What was observed

Arc Chan identifies itself through [archan.vip](https://archan.vip/), [@archanmeme](https://x.com/archanmeme), and the contract `0x8dac5f5a1c9925c3d8fafa1e87a831804faf45c4`. The official site presents a consistent anime character, six visual variants, an Arc-chain identity, a visible contract, and a four-step wallet/chain/venue/chart path. It also labels the project a community memecoin and includes a “Nothing here is financial advice” notice.

[Argus](https://argus.world/token/0x8dac5f5a1c9925c3d8fafa1e87a831804faf45c4) was observed showing 100% graduated at roughly 20 hours after the displayed launch. The exact graduation transaction and minute were not independently recovered. This supports the narrow observation “graduated within about 20 hours,” not “marketing caused success.”

The visible X sequence included an Arc teaser, build invitation, [launch/contract post](https://x.com/archanmeme/status/2103946791226114551), CoinGecko application and listing announcements, a [DEX update](https://x.com/archanmeme/status/2103965591791092073), a [short character video](https://x.com/archanmeme/status/2103975429707284747), and an image follow-up. Listing announcements are project claims; processing times were not independently verified. Displayed reactions and views are snapshots; follower origin, paid promotion, and referral paths were not verified. The website's presence at our observation time does not establish that every feature existed before graduation.

Tracker pages disagreed materially across fetches on holders, liquidity, FDV, and volume. Those values are therefore not combined into a price or market-cap timeline. “Graduated” also does not establish durable price retention, organic demand, or a particular acquisition channel.

## Transferable lessons for J7C

1. Establish one memorable character, palette, and recurring visual formats before increasing posting frequency.
2. Make verification and discovery easy with a single accurate destination for the contract, project page, and official account.
3. Use genuine release events and useful community prompts; measure substantive participants and returning contributors.
4. Treat listing, volume, price, and graduation as observations. Do not promise that a format will reproduce another token’s outcome.

J7C’s baseline milestone snapshot was 1.6% on 2026-09-27 at approximately 23:40 KST. This is a historical observation; the 100% objective has not been verified as achieved in this log. A GeckoTerminal information form was prepared but not submitted; the current route required paid review, and no payment or approval was made. See the [changelog](changelog.md) for completed changes and their evidence.

Next hypothesis: coherent Joan visuals and one clear information destination may make the project easier to recognize and evaluate. We will look for substantive participation and returning contributors; market moves alone cannot establish that this hypothesis worked.
